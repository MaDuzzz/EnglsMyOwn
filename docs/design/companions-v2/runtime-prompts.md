# Runtime atlas prompts

Tool: built-in Imagegen. Transparent output attempts were discarded. The shipped opaque source is processed by the app during rendering.

## Cat

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: PRODUCTION CHROMA-KEY SPRITE ATLAS for a small animated companion in a web app.
Input 1 is the user's approved five-stage growth board. Extract and faithfully preserve its five animals' character identities, anatomy, faces, fur, mature morphology, palette and cushions. No redesign. It is essential that the mature adult has the detailed mature facial and body characteristics from the reference, not an enlarged baby.
Output EXACTLY FIVE COLUMNS by THREE ROWS, fifteen full-body character-and-cushion sprites in a perfectly regular grid. Canvas aspect ratio exactly 5:3. All fifteen cells are equal SQUARE tiles. Grid edges at exactly x = 0%,20%,40%,60%,80%,100% and y = 0%,33.333%,66.667%,100%. NO extra outer margin, NO heading, NO text, NO labels, NO dividers or borders, NO checker pattern, NO colored segmentation visualization. Background: perfectly flat opaque chroma green #00FF00 behind every sprite, including all empty tile margins. No gradients, no texture, no black shadows in the green. This is a source atlas for runtime color-key transparency.
Each column is one specific age: Newborn, Baby, Junior, Young adult, Adult, in that order. The five columns correspond directly to the five subjects in the reference. Keep the reference's real size progression, with a truly small sleeping newborn.
Each ROW is a facial expression only:
ROW 1 (top): resting, gentle open eyes for ages 2–5; newborn asleep.
ROW 2 (middle): both eyelids gently fully closed for a brief blink, mouth still relaxed. Newborn remains softly asleep.
ROW 3 (bottom): happy when petted, eyes curved into smiling closed crescents, subtle cheek lift, tiny natural closed-mouth smile. Newborn gives a sleepy contented face. No toothy grin, no human lips, no emoji strokes.
CRUCIAL alignment invariant: Within each COLUMN all three rows must repeat EXACTLY the same body, head position/angle, fur outline, ears, paws, tail, cushion, lighting, camera, scale and framing; only eyelids, cheeks and mouth corners change. Do not tilt the head, move paws or stretch the body for a smile. These frames will be overlaid in the same position during animation, so body drift is unacceptable.
Tile placement: same oval slate-blue cushion in EVERY tile, 84% of tile width, centered horizontally, cushion baseline at 91% of tile height; full adult fits above with 5% top space. Same camera and scale within each column across rows. Every entire animal, tail, cushion and its subtle contact shadow must be contained in its own square tile, with no spill across cell boundaries. Fine fur edges must have no green reflected light, no green tint or colored outline. Render character and cushion fully opaque.
Keep the soft, near-real premium 3D-rendered style and rich fine fur from the approved reference. Maintain continuous anatomically sculpted shapes, no primitive toy look. Render very high resolution with clean detailed facial expressions. Do not add clothing, accessories, hearts, props or ambient colored glow.
Species: cream domestic cat. Copy the approved v2 cat shapes: slender silky Young adult and full-bodied Adult with mature cheeks, a layered cream chest ruff and very fluffy plume tail. Keep the amber eyes and pink nose, same subtle forehead markings.
