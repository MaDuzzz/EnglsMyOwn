## Cat

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a character growth visual-development sheet for EnglsMyOwn, to approve art before implementation.
Input image 1: approved character identity and rendering style reference. Preserve the selected animal's identity, specific coat palette, recognizable markings, soft tactile fine fur, natural eyes, sculpted anatomical continuous forms, warm quiet studio light, cream background and slate-blue plush cushion material.
Composition: ONE landscape artboard, five evenly spaced equal-width columns in a single horizontal sequence. One FULL BODY animal per column. Small title at top, fine unobtrusive dividers, clean sophisticated sans-serif labels below each animal. Exact stage labels left to right: "Newborn", "Baby", "Junior", "Young adult", "Adult". No dates, points, clocks, phones, arrows, UI, badges or additional animals.
Each column uses an IDENTICAL fixed camera distance and identical oval slate-blue cushion with the SAME size in the picture; do NOT enlarge the newborn to fill its cell. Anchor all cushions and animals at the same horizontal ground line. Growth must be visible as actual increasing body size relative to the fixed cushion, alongside meaningful changing anatomy, fur and facial proportions. Leave generous headroom for adult ears/head. Entire cushions, paws, tails and fur must be inside their cells. Use a wide enough landscape canvas for five crisp beautifully detailed characters and readable labels.
Stage progression:
1 NEWBORN: minuscule just-born baby, body about one fifth of adult height, curled or belly-resting low on the cushion, folded/soft baby ears appropriate for species, closed or barely open eyes, sparse very short fine newborn fuzz (not hairless, not sickly), pinkish delicate skin at ears and paws. Truly tiny, do not show a mature fluffy kitten here.
2 BABY: closely match the approved baby from the reference, tiny short-limbed rounded body, softly oversized head, gentle open curious eyes, newly fluffy fine baby fur, relaxed seated pose. Body about 40 percent adult height.
3 JUNIOR: about 65 percent adult height, longer legs and torso, head proportion gradually less oversized, slightly finer young face, fur filling out, friendly attentive seated pose.
4 YOUNG ADULT: about 85 percent adult height, clear emerging adult proportions, fuller balanced body, mature coat emerging, still affectionate youthful expression.
5 ADULT: full grown beautiful healthy companion, species-correct adult silhouette and face proportions, rich mature coat, kind reassuring natural eyes, friendly calm closed mouth. Adult does NOT mean elderly, giant cartoon head or aggressive teeth.
Progression should look like the SAME beloved individual growing slowly, not five breeds. Naturalistic but charming premium soft 3D creature rendering with detailed fur, no primitive sphere construction, no plastic shine, no muscular fantasy beast. Avoid making the stages only scaled copies or giving every stage the same face.
